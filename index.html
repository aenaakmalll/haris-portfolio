Orroland

A direct-booking website for a magical estate

Orroland is a landscape of woodlands and sculptures in Dumfries & Galloway, with secluded self-catering cottages. This case study explores how a website can communicate a destination’s character and make the path from discovery to booking clearer.

The challenge

The existing online presence needed to better express what makes Orroland special. The opportunity was to present the estate as a destination in its own right and make direct enquiries easier to find, rather than relying only on online travel agencies.

A clearer website journey

A well-organized page structure and clear calls to action help visitors move from discovering the estate to exploring the cottages and making an enquiry. The experience should feel calm, informative, and straightforward on desktop and mobile.

Content and visual storytelling

Photography and illustration help build a sense of place, communicate the estate’s character, and give potential guests a stronger feel for the experience before they arrive. The visual direction takes cues from storybook imagery and the estate’s handcrafted details.

Ardoe House Hotel & Spa

A renewed identity for a historic baronial mansion

Ardoe House Hotel & Spa is a historic mansion in Aberdeen. This reference study looks at how a refreshed identity and digital presence can respect a property’s heritage while helping it connect with contemporary audiences.

The challenge

The hotel needed a clear and distinctive presentation that could reconnect with local audiences while appealing to visitors planning weddings, celebrations, and relaxing stays. Its digital experience needed to balance the building’s history with an easy-to-navigate modern feel.

Positioning the brand

The brand story draws on the property’s heritage and the memories associated with it, from weddings and special occasions to guest experiences. A consistent tone and visual direction help communicate its character across different touchpoints.

A cohesive visual system

Developed in WordPress, the website brings the design and content together in a manageable, responsive setup. The layouts adapt to desktop and mobile screens, allowing visitors to explore the restaurant's online presence across devices.
